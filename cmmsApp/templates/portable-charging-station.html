PORTABLE DC CHARGER

Charge anywhere with iPower Portable DC Chargers

Portable DC Chargers are available in 10kW, 20kW & 50kW. These quick DC charging stations give EV owners the opportunity to charge safely and fast ideal for convenience and emergency charging. A typical EV with a 24kWh battery pack can be charged to ~80% in under 1.5 hours (vehicle dependent).

iPower Portable DC Charger combines industry standardisation with advanced charging technology to support next-generation electric vehicles. Its multi-protocol design allows easy tailoring to support CHAdeMO and CCS standards for DC fast charging applications.

With all great ideas, they appear nowhere then everywhere at the same time as people around the world devise solutions to looming challenges. One of those solutions is portable power banks for emergency and/or convenience automobile charging.

iPower is ideal for consumers who want an EV but are concerned about range, and for those who live or work where there is no allocated off-street parking with a charging station.

Portable charging boosts flexibility and convenience, making it easier to transition to electric vehicles. The user can recharge the unit at home or at work, then transport it to the vehicle much like a compact suitcase enabling charging from virtually any parking space. In many cases, recharging from the grid can also be more cost-effective than public charging.

It’s compact enough to fit in a car boot, and designed for easy movement with a retractable handle and wheels simple to move between car, home, and workplace.
