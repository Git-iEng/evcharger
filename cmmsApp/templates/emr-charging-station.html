EMERGENCY MOBILE EV CHARGER

Easy charging power everywhere

iPower Emergency mobile EV charger is the perfect solution to provide Fast DC Charging for roadside assistance on electric vehicles or for battery operated tools & equipment used in remote areas.

Our Emergency EV charging system can be mounted in a van/ute or truck, and driven to a power-run-out electric vehicle for emergency charging. iPower Mobile EV charger complies with AS/NZS 3000, AS/IEC 61851, and AS/IEC 62196.2 standards.

EV Charger Applications & Features

  • Fast charging as roadside assistance for electric vehicles
  • Emergency charging of battery-operated tools in remote areas
  • Battery Management System
  • Various available sockets/plugs: CCS1, CCS2, CHAdeMO, or Tesla
  • Easy to install or mount in an emergency roadside assistance vehicle

OUR WORK

Emergency Mobile EV Charger

Emergency Mobile EV Charger 60 kW delivers fast DC charging wherever it’s needed—ideal for roadside assistance, depots, and remote locations.

Charger input

  • iPower charging system’s 56 kWh battery pack can be charged at any public EV charging station (AC/DC) with CCS2 or CCS1, CHAdeMO, Type 2, or Type 1 plugs/cables once battery level is low.

Charger output

This 60 kW fast charging system can charge any EV with charging plugs/cables of CCS1 or CCS2, CHAdeMO, or Tesla.
