AC CHARGER

Charge smarter every day with our AC Charger solutions.

Electric mobility is growing rapidly across Australia, and dependable everyday charging is essential to support drivers at home, at work and across public destinations. Our AC chargers are built to deliver safe, accessible charging that helps communities transition to cleaner transport.

Why AC Charger? We are pleased to share that our AC chargers have been approved by the NSW Electric Vehicle Council. This approval reflects our focus on delivering high-quality EV charging solutions for a wide range of applications and helps support the growing demand for sustainable transportation infrastructure in New South Wales.

To assist our e-mobility customers, iEngineering Group provides high-quality iPower series AC charging solutions as a turnkey option for businesses, councils and property owners. Our chargers are designed exclusively for AC charging of electric vehicles and are built for reliable day-to-day use.

Our AC charging stations are designed for versatility and can be installed in locations such as supermarkets, shopping centres, railway stations, service stations, council or business parking lots, public parking areas, cafes, restaurants, resorts, residential complexes and more. By providing accessible charging where people live, work and shop, organisations can support EV adoption with confidence.

iEngineering Group offers EV AC chargers in 7kW, 22kW, and 44kW variants. These models are available in single-phase and three-phase configurations, allowing you to match the charger to the site’s electrical capacity and expected usage.

Built with industrial-grade designs, our AC chargers are dustproof and waterproof with an IP65 protection rating, supporting safe and efficient outdoor operation. This makes them well-suited for public areas, workplaces, fleet parking and other high-use environments.

AC charging is typically ideal for longer dwell times, such as overnight residential charging or daytime charging at workplaces and destinations. It provides a cost-effective and practical solution for daily top-ups, helping drivers maintain range without needing fast-charging infrastructure at every location.

Contactor Adhesion Protection

When a contactor is stuck due to current, wires in a socket type charger may remain live and create a risk of electric shock. Chargers with Contactor Adhesion Protection help avoid this danger.

SMART HOME

Dynamic Load Balancing (DLB)

DLB is available for home use. When the EV charger works with other household appliances at the same time, it maintains a dynamic balance of the total household current and supports safe operation to help avoid home overload.
