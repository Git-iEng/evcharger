About us

About us – where vision meets execution!

iEngineering Australia delivers advanced power system analysis and electrical engineering solutions designed to support safe, reliable, and efficient energy networks. We work with utilities, industrial operators, and infrastructure owners across multiple sectors to provide accurate studies, compliance-driven designs, and data-backed engineering insights. With a strong emphasis on technical excellence, regulatory alignment, and long-term system resilience, our mission is to help organizations plan, operate, and expand electrical networks with confidence while supporting sustainable and future-ready power systems.

Our Mission

At iEngineering Australia, our mission is to enhance efficiency, ensure quality control, and deliver customer satisfaction through innovative engineering solutions. We are committed to global standards and sustainable solutions that create long-term value for our clients and communities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a global leader in engineering solutions, driving industry leadership, innovation, and customer-centric growth. We aim to inspire through global influence, foster expansion and growth, and play an inspirational role in shaping the future of engineering.

Our Values

Our values guide everything we do: integrity & ethics, a strong commitment to quality, and diversity & inclusion. Safety, reliability, and continuous improvement are the cornerstones of our success, enabling us to deliver trusted and innovative solutions worldwide.

Our Journey

Founded in 2013, iEngineering Australia delivers a comprehensive range of high-quality electrical and power system design services tailored for Australian and Pacific network conditions. From concept development and detailed engineering to modelling, verification, and commissioning support, we ensure every design whether for substations, earthing and lightning systems, renewable energy plants, or EV infrastructure is compliant, robust, and engineered for long-term operational reliability.
